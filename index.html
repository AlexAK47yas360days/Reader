Getting more voices

This reader speaks using the voices already installed on your device — there's no in-page download, because browsers don't allow a webpage to install system voices directly. Adding one takes a minute in your device's settings, and it'll show up here automatically.

  1. Open Settings → Time & Language → Speech.
  2. Under "Manage voices," click Add voices and pick a language/accent.
  3. Come back here and press Refresh voices below.

Tip: Microsoft Edge also ships its own "Natural" voices that are noticeably more lifelike than the default Windows ones — if you're not on Edge, opening this page there is the fastest upgrade.

  1. Open System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content.
  2. Click the System Voice dropdown → Manage Voices…
  3. Download any voice — the ones labelled "Premium" or "Enhanced" sound the most natural.
  4. Come back here and press Refresh voices below.
